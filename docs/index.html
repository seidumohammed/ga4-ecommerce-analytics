About the data. The shop, customers, products and numbers are invented. The events follow the layout of the GA4 BigQuery export, and the raw data has known problems planted on purpose (duplicates, late events, bots, internal traffic, double-fired purchases, spelling differences in ad spend). Patterns such as the Black Friday peak and the mobile conversion gap were built into the generator, so they show what the pipeline can surface, not what a real shop would find.

Definitions. Session: a user plus a GA4 session id. Engaged session: 10 seconds of engagement, 2 page views or a purchase. Conversion rate: sessions with a purchase divided by sessions. Revenue: order value including shipping, dated by the day the session started. Return on ad spend: revenue divided by ad spend for the same dates.
